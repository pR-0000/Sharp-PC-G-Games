Pocket Rally takes inspiration from the overhead racing and scrolling tilemaps of **[Tiny CarZ](https://www.ticalc.org/archives/files/fileinfo/391/39185.html)**, created by **Léo Ducas / Kuda-Soft** for TI-83 Plus.

Pocket Rally's Sharp Z80 code, tilemapper, graphics, circuits and tools are original work by P.RAIMBAULT, distributed under [MIT](../../LICENSE.txt). No code, binaries, circuits, artwork or tools from the Tiny CarZ archive are redistributed with this project. Tiny CarZ retains its author's rights and conditions.

Sharp hardware and IOCS references are listed in the [shared include](../../include/sharp-pc-g8xx.inc). Sprite composition routines come from this collection's MIT-licensed Pocket Jumper code.
