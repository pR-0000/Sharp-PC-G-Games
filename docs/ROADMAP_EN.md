# Possible additions

[Français](ROADMAP.md) · **English** · [Collection](../README.md#english)

Possible future additions include Sokoban-style puzzles, a brick breaker and Snake. Pocket Jumper could gain more levels and a level editor. These are design ideas, rather than included games or announced releases.

[Pocket Rally](../games/pocket-rally/README.md#english) adds overhead racing and a two-axis scrolling tilemapper to the collection.
